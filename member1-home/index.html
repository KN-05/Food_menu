A neighbourhood kitchen since 2014

Honest food, cooked slow, served warm.

Olive & Gold is a small kitchen built around one idea: good ingredients need very little help. We roast, simmer and bake in small batches every morning, and we keep the plates simple so the produce can do the talking.

Drop in for breakfast, linger over lunch, or book a table for a slow dinner with people you like. Everything on the menu is made in-house, right down to the bread.
